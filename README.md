# Kukiz's Tuxedo Cat Texture Pack

A small resource pack inspired by me wanting my own cats in the game and giving a few Minecraft textures a new look!

The pack keeps the vanilla Minecraft style while replacing selected cat-related textures and the Totem of Undying with cat textures that are designed to recreate the fur patterns and markings of my real cats while still fitting naturally into Minecraft's vanilla style.

## What it changes

### Adult Tuxedo Cat
The vanilla black-and-white cat variant is replaced with a custom tuxedo-style texture based on one of my cats.

![Cat](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/ac3d106a6fd8be3d2d2287895484abbc5008a304.png)

---

### Baby Tuxedo Cat
With Minecraft 26.1 introducing separate baby cat textures, the baby tuxedo cat has its own custom texture designed to match the adult tuxedo cat.

![Baby Tuxedo Cat](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/bf44b71f4d15b6c4ae99bbb1a5473a12d12301bb.png)

---

### Adult Jellie Cat
A custom black-and-white texture for the Jellie cat based on another one of my cats.

![Adult Jellie](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/b4324f19eeb960e7e55264b25de718d8938fb838.png)

---

### Baby Jellie Cat
A matching baby texture based on the adult Jellie cat.

![Baby Jellie](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/4731d1e70593674295a18ae20e105d78b84ee088.png)

---

### Totem of Undying
The Totem of Undying is retextured into a small tuxedo cat, matching the style of the rest of the pack.

![Totem Of Undying](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/fa69b84901c74e3da0402aa8daeee915ca28ffa1.png)

---

### Cat Spawn Egg
The Cat Spawn Egg uses a custom tuxedo cat-inspired texture to fit the rest of the pack.

![Cat Spawn Egg](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/d95b44ec1d2c592db08e815bf2aba19afdd79ef9_0.webp)

## Style

The goal is to keep everything feeling close to vanilla Minecraft while adding a small personal touch by bringing my own cats into the game.
