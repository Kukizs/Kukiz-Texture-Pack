# Kukiz's Texture Pack

A small resource pack inspired by wanting my own cats in Minecraft!

The pack recreates the fur patterns and markings of my real cats — **Kukiz, Lily, and Luna** — while keeping Minecraft’s vanilla style. Each cat has a matching baby texture, alongside a custom Totem of Undying and Cat Spawn Egg.

## Optional ETF Support

![ETF](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/b3cecebd7e7b4d42823f9ddd8b0d99c2a986a442.png)

With **[Entity Texture Features (ETF)](https://modrinth.com/mod/entitytexturefeatures)** installed, the vanilla tuxedo, Jellie, and Persian appearances can appear alongside my custom textures.

Each of these three cat types has a **50/50 chance** of using its vanilla or custom appearance, including babies. Other cat types keep their normal appearances.

**ETF is optional.** Without it, the pack continues to replace the tuxedo, Jellie, and Persian textures normally.

### Name-Tag Textures ![Name Tag](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/5837940c2c085ccedd03047d22ed5d147f398de7.png)

With ETF installed, naming **any cat** one of the following gives it that cat’s custom appearance:

- **Kukiz** — custom tuxedo cat.
- **Lily** — custom Jellie cat.
- **Luna** — custom Persian cat.

These work for both adults and babies. **Names are case-sensitive**, so use them exactly as written above.

## Style

The goal is to keep everything feeling close to vanilla Minecraft while adding a small personal touch by bringing my own cats into the game.
